LocalGo Privacy Policy

Updated October 8, 2026 · LocalGo consumer app

LocalGo is operated by Samer Odeh. This policy explains how LocalGo handles personal information when you use the consumer app or contact support.

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or account-deletion requests, contact samerodeh.dev@gmail.com.

Information we handle

Account information. When you create an account, we handle your first and last name, email address, account identifier, and authentication information. Supabase provides account authentication. Your password is sent to the authentication service to create or access your account. The app stores authentication session information on your device to keep you signed in.

Addresses and location. You can enter a delivery address, unit or apartment details, and delivery instructions. If you choose to use your current location and grant permission, the app accesses your location to help fill in your address. Address searches are sent to the Photon geocoding service at photon.komoot.io. When you use location-based address lookup, coordinates are sent to that service. LocalGo does not request background location access in the current Android consumer app.

Cart and order information. The app handles selected menu items, quantities, totals, delivery details, and any order records associated with your use of enabled ordering features. Saved addresses and order history may be stored locally on your device. When ordering is enabled, relevant order information is sent to the backend and associated service providers to arrange and track delivery. Ordering and payment are currently unavailable in the current consumer test release.

Messages and attachments. If you use an available order conversation, we handle messages and any photos or voice recordings you choose to send. Microphone access is used when recording a voice message. Selecting a photo lets the app upload the selected item. Messages and attachments are made available to the conversation participants. In the current implementation, uploaded attachments use publicly accessible storage links: anyone who obtains a link may access that attachment. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or other sensitive material through chat.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we may associate a push-notification token and device platform with your account to deliver relevant alerts. Expo and platform notification services process information needed to route those alerts.

Support and technical information. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. Network requests to our hosting, authentication, geocoding, and image services expose technical information such as your IP address to those services. Service providers may process request and error logs to operate and protect their services. Restaurant and menu images are loaded from external image hosts.

How we use information

We use information to provide accounts and sign-in, display menus, manage carts and addresses, determine whether an address is in the service area, support enabled order and messaging features, send notifications you permit, answer support requests, and diagnose or prevent service problems and misuse.

The current app does not display advertising or include an advertising SDK. If the app's features or data practices change, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures.

Who receives information

LocalGo uses Supabase for authentication, database, realtime messaging, and attachment storage; a backend hosted on Render; Photon for address lookup; and Expo/platform services for notifications. External restaurant-image hosts receive requests for the images displayed in the app. These providers process information needed for the services they supply.

When delivery features are enabled, the driver handling an order receives the customer and delivery information necessary to fulfill it. Messages and attachments are available to their intended participants, subject to the storage-link limitation described above. We may also disclose information when legally required or where necessary to address fraud, misuse, or a security incident.

The current test release does not accept payments. Before enabling payments, we will update this policy to describe the payment provider and the information processed by that integration.

Storage, retention, and deletion

LocalGo stores some information on your device and some with its service providers. Hosting and processing may occur outside Canada; the available protections and applicable laws can differ by location.

We retain information for the time needed to provide the relevant service, maintain your account, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. When you request account deletion, we verify the request and remove your account and associated personal information that is no longer required. If particular records must be retained for a legal obligation or a specific unresolved issue, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period in our response. Copies in backups may remain until the relevant backup retention period expires.

You can request deletion from Settings → Delete Account in the app or on our account-deletion page. To request deletion directly, email samerodeh.dev@gmail.com from the email address registered to your LocalGo account with the subject Delete my LocalGo account. Do not include your password. Removing the app from your phone does not by itself delete the account stored with our services. You can remove locally stored app data through Android's app settings.

Your choices

You can decline or withdraw location, microphone, photo, and notification permissions through your device settings. Features that require a permission may be unavailable when you decline it. You can enter an address manually rather than use current-location lookup, although entered addresses still use the geocoding service for validation. You can contact us to request access to or correction of personal information and to ask about withdrawing consent or deleting your account.

Younger users

LocalGo is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not designed specifically for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through LocalGo, contact us so we can investigate and address it. This audience statement does not claim that the current app verifies users' ages.

Security

We use authentication and access controls in the app and its services, and the configured hosted services use HTTPS for network connections. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed secure. The current attachment-link design is described above so that users can make an informed choice about what they send.

Changes and contact

We will revise this policy when our practices change and update its effective date. For questions or requests, contact Samer Odeh, LocalGo — samerodeh.dev@gmail.com.
