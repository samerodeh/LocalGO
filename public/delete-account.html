Delete your LocalGo account

Updated October 8, 2026 · LocalGo consumer app

You can request deletion of your LocalGo consumer account and its associated personal information without installing the app.

Send a deletion request

Email samerodeh.dev@gmail.com from the email address you use for LocalGo.

  • Subject: Delete my LocalGo account
  • Message: Please delete my LocalGo consumer account and the personal information associated with it.

Open an email deletion request

Do not send your password, payment-card information, or identity documents. If you no longer have access to your registered email address, contact the same support address for help verifying ownership.

What happens next

We verify that the request comes from the account owner and respond with the expected completion time. Deletion covers the consumer account and associated profile information, saved server-side delivery details, push tokens, and other personal information we no longer need to retain, including eligible order, message, and attachment records.

If a specific record must be retained to meet a legal obligation or resolve an outstanding issue, we will explain the reason and retention period in our response. Backup copies may remain until the relevant backup retention period expires. Copies of messages or attachments already saved by other participants cannot be removed from their devices by deleting your LocalGo account.

Uninstalling LocalGo does not delete your server-side account. To remove data stored on your Android phone, use Settings → Apps → LocalGo → Storage → Clear storage, or the equivalent option on your device.
